## Projects

### [HireHelper](https://github.com/BytesBySuyash/HireHelper) — a little help from your neighbours

A task assistance app where people can post everyday tasks, offer to help, and follow the work through to completion.

The project includes a full backend, authentication, notifications, and tests. A key part of the workflow is making sure only one helper gets assigned and the task owner confirms when the work is done.

**Angular · NestJS · PostgreSQL · Prisma**

[Try the demo](https://bytesbysuyash.github.io/HireHelper/) · [Read the code](https://github.com/BytesBySuyash/HireHelper)

### [ReClaim](https://github.com/BytesBySuyash/ReClaim) — looking at recovery from every side

A disaster recovery coordination demo with connected views for residents, field workers, organizations, and government teams.

It explores how reports, household information, maps, and recovery plans could fit together. This is a frontend prototype with sample data; submissions and analysis are simulated.

**React · TypeScript · Tailwind CSS · Leaflet**

[Explore the demo](https://bytesbysuyash.github.io/ReClaim/) · [Read the code](https://github.com/BytesBySuyash/ReClaim)
